# 1. INTRODUCTION

`LifeVector` is an open-source, offline-first **self-management planner for Linux** based on **role-based weekly planning and Stephen R. Covey's principle of "Put First Things First."**

Unlike conventional task and time-management applications, `LifeVector` focuses on helping the user decide **what deserves attention and time before deciding when to do it**.

The application combines:

* Personal mission statement
* Life roles and responsibilities
* Goals and supporting activities
* Quadrant II prioritization
* Weekly priority selection
* Day allocation
* Exact scheduling
* Guided weekly planning

The central idea is:

> **Manage yourself and your priorities, rather than simply managing a list of tasks or filling a calendar.**

The application follows a planning progression:

`Mission → Roles → Goals → Prioritize → Weekly Plan → Allocate to Days → Schedule`

Exact scheduling is therefore the final step of planning rather than the starting point.
